The 12 Coins Puzzle:
find the fake coin.

12 coins, 1 fake.
A balance scale.
Only 3 weighings.

Most say: weigh
pairs one by one.

That takes up to
6 weighings, not 3.

A scale gives 3
outcomes, not 2:
left, right, even.

3 weighings give
3³ = 27 outcomes.

12 coins, 2 states
each = 24 to find.

27 ≥ 24, so 3
weighings is enough.

Split into groups
of 4. Weigh 4 v 4.

Count outcomes,
not comparisons.
