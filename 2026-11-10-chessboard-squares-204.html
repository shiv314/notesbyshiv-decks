PUZZLE

Chessboard squares:
how many squares are
on a chessboard?

A normal 8 × 8 board.
Squares of any size.
Count them all.

64.

The answer almost everyone gives first.

64 counts only
the 1 × 1 squares.

The whole board is a square too. So is every 2 × 2 block.

Count corners,
not squares.

Every square has exactly one top-left corner. Count where that corner can sit.

A 2 × 2 fits in
7 × 7 = 49 spots.

Its top-left corner can't sit in the last row or the last column.

The 8 × 8 fits
in exactly one.

Bigger square, fewer places to put it.

204 squares.

64 + 49 + 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1. The first eight square numbers, added up.

Count each thing
by one anchor.

Pick a point every object has exactly one of, and overlaps stop being a problem. Rectangles on the same board: 1,296.
